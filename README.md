# Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

The **Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis** project analyzes agricultural data across the **Kharif, Rabi and Zaid** seasons to identify patterns and variations in crop productivity, resource usage, environmental conditions, profitability and regional performance.

The project uses Python-based data analysis and visualization techniques to transform agricultural data into meaningful insights that can support better seasonal planning and agricultural decision-making.

## Objectives

* Compare agricultural performance across different seasons.
* Analyze crop yield and production patterns.
* Study irrigation methods and water efficiency.
* Examine environmental conditions and disease/pest risk.
* Analyze revenue, cost and profitability.
* Identify regional variations across states.
* Study relationships between agricultural variables using correlation and statistical analysis.
* Identify unusual observations and outliers.

## Key Findings

* **Kharif** recorded the highest average yield at **5.63 tonnes/ha**.
* **Sugarcane** recorded the highest average crop yield at **46.64 tonnes/ha**.
* **Drip irrigation** recorded the highest average yield and profit among the analyzed irrigation methods.
* Kharif showed the highest average water efficiency.
* Kharif had the strongest overall profitability, while Zaid showed the highest proportion of loss-making farms.
* Water efficiency showed a strong positive relationship with yield.
* Regional and seasonal variations were observed across different states.
